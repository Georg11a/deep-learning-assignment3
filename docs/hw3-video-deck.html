With the centred scorer, the attention weights barely moved under volume changes, yet answers still flipped. A position embedding recovered part of the accuracy (53.1%).

The damage happens before attention

For every clip a volume change turned from correct to wrong, I swapped one part at a time from the louder or quieter version.

Adjusting only the weights was not enough. Two loudness features shift the segment vectors before attention combines them.

If you change a part and the errors don’t move, you changed the wrong part

What worked: a swap test located the problem, and centring two input features fixed the volume flips with almost no change in accuracy.

What I learned: before asking a model to use a cue, decide what the cue is compared with, and whose judgement the model should match: the actor's intent or what listeners hear. Stable is not correct; the model still misses about 45% of clips.
